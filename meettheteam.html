The people behind StudyPrep

M e e t   U s .

Five students working together to make exam preparation easier to find, easier to understand, and accessible to everyone.

01

Satvika Dudala

Co-Founder · Project Manager

I am Satvika Dudala, a Kode With Klossy Web Development Scholar and co-founder of StudyPrep Resources. The project began after I spent more time searching for trustworthy SAT resources than actually studying them. This problem was rising among many lost high school students and we hope our website could battle against it.

Building StudyPrep taught me how to manage, organize, lead, and collaborate with a thoughtful team. We hope the resources collected here help students study with more confidence and less unnecessary stress.

02

Dilnara Tairova

Co-Founder · Visionary

I’m Dilnara Tairova, one of the co-founders of StudyPrep Resources and a member of the class of 2030. After taking the SHSAT, I saw that expensive preparation did not always give students the help they needed.

We created this project to offer students something useful, accessible, and free.

03

Jane Cheng

Co-Founder · Creative Manager

I’m Jane Cheng, one of the creators of StudyPrep Resources. I’ve watched students struggle to find reliable materials that help them move forward.

I shaped the website’s visual direction and color system. I’m grateful for the opportunity to create something that gives more students the chance to learn and aim higher.

04

Jocelyn Jia

Co-Founder · Researcher

I’m Jocelyn Jia, class of 2029. Having taken the SHSAT myself, I understand how difficult finding useful preparation resources can be.

My research helps connect students with tools such as Excelsior Learning, which offers free virtual SHSAT tutoring during the summer.

05

Aaliyah Mansfield

Co-Founder · Developer

I’m Aaliyah Mansfield. I enjoy reading, drawing, and coding. While preparing for the SHSAT, I struggled to find dependable study resources and felt more nervous than I needed to.

I helped create StudyPrep Resources so students can build confidence without paying for information and support that should be accessible.
